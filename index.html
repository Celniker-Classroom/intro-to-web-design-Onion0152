General Infomation:

Freestyle is a stroke in which athletes can swim any stroke, although the front crawl is almost universally used for speed. The stroke is known for its alternating arm movements, flutter kick, and rhythmic breathing, which together create a fast and efficient propulsion through the water.

History:

Freestyle's modern form can be traced back to Egyptian art back in 2000 BCE, brought to western attention in the 19th century. The modern stroke was popularized by Pacific Islander swimmers, showcased by Alick Wickham from the Solomon Islands around 1901 in Australia then refined and internationally spread. The front crawl has become a default choice for freestyle events because of it's speed, making the terms interchangable in practice.

significant modern athletes:

Significant athletes specializing in freestyle include Pan Zhanle from china, the Olympic gold medalist of 2024 in the 100m freestyle with the fastest in history, Caleb Dressel from the USA, a seven-time olympic gold medalist, considered one of the greatest sprint freestylers in history, and Katie Ledecky also of USA with a dominant distance freestyle position, holding multiple world records,seven gold medals, and numerous world records.
